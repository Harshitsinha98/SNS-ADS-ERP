CodeSkate — Signature Marks

Six concept-driven marks built on one idea: motion & glide — the "skate" in CodeSkate. Each is abstract enough to front your agency, CRM & Voice AI. Shown on dark & light.

01 · Signature C

06 · Continuum
One unbroken stroke traces a figure-8 — the flowing path a skater carves, and a quiet nod to "always-on / infinite scale." Sophisticated, calm, enterprise-grade.
Designer's pick: For a company running three businesses, go with 01 Signature C as the master mark — it's the most timeless and scales flawlessly from favicon to hoarding, and stays neutral across agency, CRM & Voice. If you want more personality, 02 Glide Swoosh is the standout — it captures the "skate" motion beautifully while staying abstract. Keep the orange tile + "CodeSkate" wordmark as-is; just drop in the new mark. Products then read as CodeSkate Studio · CRM · Voice.
