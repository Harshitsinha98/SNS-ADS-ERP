Codeskate CRM — App Review: Reviewer Instructions

App ID 1581914519978056  ·  https://crm.codeskate.com  ·  Multi-tenant lead management SaaS (verified Tech Provider)

1. What the app does

Codeskate CRM is a multi-tenant lead management platform. Each business (workspace) connects its own Facebook Page and WhatsApp Business account. Leads submitted through the business's Facebook Lead Ads instant forms flow automatically into its CRM workspace and are assigned to a team member, and the business can reply to its own customers over WhatsApp from inside the CRM. Data for each business is isolated to its own workspace; access tokens are stored encrypted and never appear in lead records or logs.

3. Reviewing Facebook Lead Ads

Demonstrates leads_retrieval, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_metadata, business_management.

  1. From the sidebar, open Ads Lead Integration. The connected Facebook Page shows a “Connected” status. (Connecting a Page uses Facebook Login for Business — pages_show_list to list the admin's Pages, pages_read_engagement to confirm access, business_management for Business-owned Pages, and pages_manage_metadata to subscribe to the Page's leadgen webhook.)
  2. Open Meta's Lead Ads Testing Tool, select the connected Page and a form, delete any existing test lead, then click Create Lead.
  3. Return to the CRM Leads page. The test lead appears automatically, tagged as a “Meta Lead Ads” source, and is auto-assigned to a team member. This demonstrates leads_retrieval: on the leadgen webhook, the app fetches the lead's name, phone, and email and creates the CRM record.

4. Reviewing WhatsApp

Demonstrates whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management.

  1. Open the WhatsApp section to view the connected phone number and the account's message templates. This demonstrates whatsapp_business_management (reading connected phone numbers and reading/managing templates).
  2. Open a customer conversation and send a reply within the 24-hour customer service window. This demonstrates whatsapp_business_messaging. Messages are only sent to customers who have already contacted the business.

5. Permission-to-feature map

public_profileIdentifies the logged-in admin during Facebook Login.

6. Data handling summary

  • Platform Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (asia-south1) with per-workspace tenant separation and role-based access control.
  • Integration access tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and never appear in lead records or logs.
  • All incoming webhooks are verified with signature validation before processing.
  • Data is used only to deliver a business's own leads and messages into its own workspace. It is never sold and never used for advertising or audience building.
  • Deletion can be requested at https://crm.codeskate.com/data-deletion and is processed within 30 days.
