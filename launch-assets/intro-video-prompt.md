1. The **launch banners** (3 sizes) rendered from your live brand system.
2. A **master video prompt** written in the Apple keynote / product-film style.

Everything below is drawn from your actual product — the landing page copy, the

## 1. Master prompt (paste into an AI video generator)

> **A 30-second premium product launch film in the style of an Apple keynote
> reveal.** Cinematic, minimal, calm and confident. A warm near-black studio
> (#0F0C0A) with a single soft orange bloom of light (#FF6B1A) drifting in the
> background like a slow aurora. Shallow depth of field, slow dolly moves,
> smooth 24fps motion, subtle film grain, no clutter.
>
> The hero object is a floating glass smartphone showing a WhatsApp-style chat.
> Messages arrive and an AI reply types itself out instantly and glows orange.
> The camera glides across clean floating UI cards — a lead inbox, a call being
> auto-logged, a live analytics chart rising — each lit by warm orange rim
> light against the dark.
>
> Typography is large, bold Space Grotesk in white with key words in an
> orange-to-ember gradient. Text animates in with a gentle fade-and-rise. The
> brand mark — a rounded orange tile with a white "C" arc — assembles itself in
> the final shot beside the words "CodeSkate CRM".
>
> Tone: aspirational, quiet luxury, tech-forward. Color palette strictly warm
> orange, ember, and near-black. Lighting soft and directional. Absolutely no
> stock-corporate feel, no busy transitions, no bright white backgrounds.

Keep this as your "style anchor" and feed the shot list below one scene at a
time (most AI video tools produce the best results on 5–8 second clips, which
you then stitch together).

## 2. Shot-by-shot script (30s)

Each block = one clip. The **Prompt** line is what you paste; the **On-screen
text** is added in your editor (or via the tool's text feature).

**Scene 1 — Cold open (0–4s)**
- Prompt: *Extreme slow push-in through darkness toward a single warm orange glow in a black studio. Dust and faint grain in the air. Cinematic, moody, anticipatory. 24fps, shallow focus.*
- On-screen text: *(none — just the glow)*
- VO/caption: "Every day, leads message your business…"

**Scene 2 — The problem (4–9s)**
- Prompt: *A floating glass smartphone appears in the dark, screen showing unread WhatsApp messages piling up. The notifications dim and fade, suggesting missed replies. Soft orange rim light. Slow orbit around the phone.*
- On-screen text: **"…and most never get a reply."**

**Scene 3 — The reveal (9–15s)**
- Prompt: *The same phone brightens. An AI reply types itself out in the chat and glows warm orange as it sends — instantly, confidently. The screen reflects orange light into the dark room. Smooth, premium.*
- On-screen text: **"Reply to every lead,"** → **"automatically."** (automatically in orange gradient)

**Scene 4 — The product (15–23s)**
- Prompt: *Camera glides sideways past three floating UI cards lit by orange rim light against black: (1) a lead inbox auto-assigning a new lead, (2) a phone call being auto-logged onto a timeline, (3) a line chart rising. Clean, minimal, depth of field.*
- On-screen text (one per card): **"AI auto-reply, 24/7"** · **"Calls logged automatically"** · **"Pipeline, live."**

**Scene 5 — Human + AI (23–27s)**
- Prompt: *Close-up of the chat as the AI hands off to a human agent — a subtle avatar glow appears, the conversation continues seamlessly. Warm, reassuring light.*
- On-screen text: **"AI handles routine. Your team handles what matters."**

**Scene 6 — Logo lock-up (27–30s)**
- Prompt: *In a clean black studio, a rounded orange tile assembles and a white "C" arc draws itself inside it, with a soft orange glow pulse. The words "CodeSkate CRM" fade in beside it. Minimal, elegant, final.*
- On-screen text: **CodeSkate CRM** — *"Reply to every lead, automatically."* — *Start free, 7 days · from ₹599/mo*

## 3. Audio direction
- Music: minimal ambient build — soft piano/pad, a single rising synth swell into Scene 3's reveal, resolve on the logo. (Apple-film restraint; no drops, no hype beat.)
- Optional VO: calm, warm, measured. 2–3 short lines only.
- Sound design: a soft "whoosh" on each UI card, a gentle chime on the AI send, a low resonant tone on the logo lock-up.

## 4. Which AI app should you use?

There's no single tool that does the whole film well, so here's how I'd actually
split it. The AI-video landscape moves fast and model names/pricing change every
few months — **verify current plans before you buy**, since even "which models
still exist" shifts (as of late 2026 roundups disagree on Sora's status).

As of the latest 2026 comparisons, the cinematic-quality leaders are **Google
Veo 3.1**, **ByteDance Seedance 2.0**, **Kling 3.0**, and **Runway Gen-4.5**.
*(Model landscape per [2026 AI-video roundups](https://felloai.com/10-best-ai-video-generators-2026-tested-ranked/); content rephrased for licensing compliance — verify before purchasing.)*

### Recommended primary: **Google Veo 3.1** (via the Gemini app / Google Flow)
- **Best for:** the cinematic hero shots (Scenes 1–3, 5), realistic lighting,
  camera moves, and **native synchronized audio/sound effects** — a big edge for
  an Apple-style film. Consistently rated top for prompt adherence + fidelity.

### Strong alternatives
- **ByteDance Seedance 2.0** — currently tops several quality leaderboards for
  photorealistic text-to-video; great for the hero product shots.
- **Kling 3.0** — best value, and strong on motion.
- **OpenAI Sora 2** — if available to you (some 2026 roundups report it
  discontinued; others still list it). Clean, physically-consistent shots and
  fast iteration if you already have a ChatGPT plan.

### For the UI / screen moments (Scene 4) — don't use text-to-video
- Record your **actual CodeSkate CRM screens** and animate them. This keeps the
  product truthful (AI video will invent fake, off-brand UI). Options:
  - **After Effects** or **Jitter / Cavalry** for motion.
  - **Runway Gen-4** if you want to stylize real footage.

### For the logo lock-up (Scene 6)
- Animate your real `SkateMark` SVG (the C-arc draw-on already exists in your
  codebase — `src/components/marketing/SkateMark.jsx`) in After Effects or
  Rive. Don't let AI redraw your logo — it won't match.

### Music & voiceover
- Music: **Suno** or **ElevenLabs Music** for a custom ambient track; or license
  from Artlist / Epidemic Sound.
- Voiceover: **ElevenLabs** (calm, warm preset).

### Easiest all-in-one (if you want one tool and less polish)
- **Canva Magic Studio** or **CapCut** — template-driven, fast, cheaper, but
  won't reach true Apple-grade cinematics.

---

### Suggested production path (practical)
1. Generate Scenes 1–3, 5, 6 in **Veo 3.1** (5–8s clips each).
2. Screen-record real CRM UI for Scene 4; animate in After Effects.
3. Make the logo lock-up from your real SVG.
4. Stitch + grade in **CapCut / Premiere / DaVinci Resolve** (warm orange grade).
5. Add Suno/ElevenLabs music + optional VO.
6. Export 16:9 (web/YouTube) and a 1:1 or 9:16 cut for Instagram/LinkedIn.
